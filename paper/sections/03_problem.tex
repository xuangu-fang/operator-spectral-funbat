\section{Problem Setting}
\label{sec:problem}

\subsection{Observation model}
We observe a continuous-indexed order-$D$ field at a sparse index set $\Obs$,
\begin{equation}
  y_i = u(x_{i1},\dots,x_{iD}) + \epsilon_i,
  \qquad \epsilon_i \sim \Norm(0,\sigma^2),
  \qquad i \in \Obs,
  \label{eq:likelihood}
\end{equation}
with $\Nobs = |\Obs|$ and $\Nobs / N_{\mathrm{full}} \le 5\%$.

\subsection{Operator-knowledge levels}
\label{sec:ladder}
Let $\{\Lop_\theta : \theta \in \Theta\}$ be an operator family, with the ground-truth field
generated by some $\theta^\star$.  The learner's information determines how the kernel bank
is built:
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lll}
  \toprule
  Level & Formally & Bank construction \\
  \midrule
  K2    & $\theta^\star$ known                       & separate $\Sop(\theta^\star)$ into $Q$ atoms \\
  K1    & $\theta^\star \in \Theta_1$, $\Theta_1$ pre-declared & draw $\theta_1..\theta_M$ from $\Theta_1$, separate each, \emph{pool} \\
  K0    & $\theta^\star \in \Theta_0 \supset \Theta_1$        & as above, wider prior \\
  K$-1$ & no operator information                    & generic dictionary \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
At K1/K0 the routing weights perform \emph{soft parameter inference}: they mix within the set
of \emph{physically reachable} spectra rather than searching an unconstrained kernel space.

\paragraph{Degeneracy boundary, stated up front.} As $\Theta$ widens to cover every
physically plausible value, the pooled bank approaches a generic dictionary and the advantage
\emph{must} vanish.  This is a falsifiable prediction, not a defect
(\Cref{sec:experiments}, P4).  The control that separates ``physically reachable subset''
from ``bigger dictionary'' is a generic bank with an \emph{identical atom count}.

\subsection{Tensor model}
\begin{equation}
  u(x_1,x_2,x_3) = \sum_{r=1}^{R} c_r\, f_{1r}(x_1) f_{2r}(x_2) f_{3r}(x_3),
  \label{eq:cp}
\end{equation}
CP rather than a dense Tucker core, to isolate the contribution of the kernel prior.
