\section{When this does not work}
\label{sec:limitations}

The method needs the operator to suppress a large part of the spectrum.  That is
not an extra assumption layered on top of low-rank tensor completion -- it is the
same condition, seen from the other side.  An operator that suppresses little
leaves a broad spectrum, and a field with a broad spectrum is not low rank, so
the host model fails before any kernel is chosen.  Consequently the failures are
predictable rather than surprising, and we screen for them before committing to a
dataset.

\paragraph{Screening criterion.}
We fit a fully observed low-rank model at the ranks we intend to use and require
its relative error to be comfortably below $1$.  Below that threshold, sparse
completion is infeasible for \emph{any} low-rank method and every arm lands near
the trivial baseline, so a kernel comparison measures nothing.  We report the
screen including what it rejected.

\paragraph{Rejected: broad-spectrum turbulence.}
Kolmogorov flow at $\mathrm{Re}=40$ needs $63\%$ of the modes along each spatial
axis for $95\%$ of the energy, and a fully observed rank-$40$ CP still leaves
$0.298$ relative error.  \todo{cite table}

\paragraph{Rejected: pattern-forming reaction-diffusion.}
PDEBench 2D diffusion-reaction shows the same $63\%$ figure, invariant to
resolution and to whether one strides or block-averages, so it is intrinsic
rather than an aliasing artefact.  Held-out NRMSE stayed above $1$ for every
combination of host model, basis, feature budget and observation ratio.

\paragraph{Rejected, and counter-intuitively so: radially band-pass operators.}
It is tempting to expect the method to shine where a generic monotone kernel
cannot follow the spectrum at all.  The opposite happens.  A linear
Swift-Hohenberg operator has its response minimised on a ring $|k| = k_0$, and a
ring is maximally non-separable: an axis-separable approximation fills the square
instead of the ring.  At $1\%$ observed the band-pass operator prior scores
$0.92$ against $0.74$ for a generic dictionary.  Separability, not
expressiveness, is what the construction requires.

\paragraph{Other limitations.}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item The evidence is on fields from a linear forced PDE.  Nonlinear and
        variable-coefficient operators are only approximated by a linearised
        constant-coefficient form, and we do not measure how far that degrades.
  \item Uncertainty is a mean-field variational posterior over a finite feature
        basis, not an exact GP posterior.
  \item The tensor is on a regular grid with a uniform random mask; structured
        sensor layouts and irregular domains are untested.
  \item The margin shrinks with observation count and is small by $5\%$ observed,
        so the method is aimed at the sparse regime and should not be sold
        outside it.
\end{enumerate}
