\subsection{Main experiment: degradation along the knowledge ladder}
\label{sec:ladder-exp}
\todo{NOT YET RUN --- design only; see docs/PAPER\_TECHNICAL\_REPORT\_ZH.md \S10}

The same ground-truth fields are reconstructed at every knowledge level, each with and
without the generic floor, against two fairness controls: an \emph{atom-count-matched}
generic bank, and a \emph{wrong-family} pooled bank of identical size.  Pre-registered,
falsifiable predictions:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item[\textbf{P1}] $\mathrm{NRMSE}(\mathrm{K2}) \le \mathrm{K1} \le \mathrm{K0} \le \mathrm{K}\!-\!1$ in the mean (K2 $\approx$ K1 permitted).
  \item[\textbf{P2}] K1 retains $\ge 50\%$ of K2's gain over K$-1$.  Below that, the ``form is enough'' claim fails and the paper retreats to a K2 positioning.
  \item[\textbf{P3}] The floor's marginal benefit \emph{grows} as the level drops.  If it is level-independent, the floor is generic regularisation, not insurance.
  \item[\textbf{P4}] K0 still measurably beats K$-1$.  If not, the physical origin has already washed out at width $\Theta_0$ --- an upper bound on applicability that must be reported.
  \item[\textbf{P5}] K1 beats the atom-count-matched generic bank.  If not, the gain is bank capacity, not physics.
  \item[\textbf{P6}] K1 beats the wrong-family pooled bank.  If not, the correct operator family is doing no work.
\end{enumerate}

P4, P5 and P6 are the most likely to fail and therefore the most informative.  Confirmation
uses \emph{fresh} seeds 301--305; seeds 201--205 are already published and count as seen.
