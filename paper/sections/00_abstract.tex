known linear operator $\Lop u = w$, the operator symbol already prescribes how energy is
distributed over joint frequencies.  We turn that joint, \emph{non-separable} spectrum into

route different spectra to different tensor modes and ranks.  Because a convex combination
of nonnegative spectra can never create frequency support that no atom has, we additionally
reserve a fixed, non-closable fraction of generic spectral mass as a support safeguard.
